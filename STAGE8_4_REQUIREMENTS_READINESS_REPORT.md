## 3. Task C — Important gaps (priority order requested; evidence)
1. **Registration & records:** no duplicate-guest rule (same guest/document can be checked in twice — observed in Stage 8.2); DOB 1800 accepted; unsaved form discarded on navigation; separate Check-in / In-house / Stay-changes pages are placeholders. *Rules cannot be decided without the URS.*
2. **Validation & consistency:** validation is client-side only and un-sourced; cross-module counts agree (functional_8_2). Not verifiable against mandated field lists.
3. **Search, reporting, dashboards:** search/filter/sort consistent and tested; report content/format requirements unknown; Export is disabled for every seeded role; non-PIL-001 figures are samples.
4. **Authentication & permissions:** **no authentication at all**; permission checks are front-end only (enforced in browser code and tested, but trivially bypassable from dev tools). Role semantics are demo roles.
5. **Government reporting & integration boundary:** everything is scripted; nothing is transmitted (proved by instrumentation in Journey 5). The acknowledgement/rejection contract, authentication to a government endpoint, retry/dedup semantics are all undefined.
6. **Auditability & operations:** audit trail is volatile (tab memory), not tamper-evident, not exportable; alerts are computed on render with no delivery, escalation or history; no real monitoring.
7. **Not demonstrable with this prototype:** persistence, multi-user concurrency, real offline operation, real PMS/government connectivity, real identity verification/OCR, performance/scale, availability, data protection controls, accessibility conformance, localisation, backup/restore.

## 5. Task E — Prototype readiness summary
**Demonstrably supports (in a browser, synthetic data, scripted outcomes):** the three-portal navigation and role-driven permissions; hotel registration → review → simulated submission → status/history; offline queue with conflict handling; government masked search with purpose, justification and audit; administration of users, roles, establishments, reference data, maker-checker configuration; simulated integration, webhook, sandbox and export behaviour that is honestly labelled and provably makes no network calls; consistent counts across portals.
**Simulated:** government interface and acknowledgements; PMS connectors; scan/OCR; integration test/health/connect/retry; webhooks; API sandbox; notifications and alerts; CSV export; "sign-in" (acting-as switch); persistence (none).
**Incomplete:** six placeholder modules; undefined business rules (duplicates, DOB, report categories, purposes, thresholds); Export disabled for all roles; no persistence.
**Not verified:** everything against the URS; other browsers/devices/assistive technology; performance; earlier-stage suites not in the package.
**External dependencies / prerequisites for any real integration (none exist yet):** the approved URS; a government interface specification (endpoint, authentication, schema, acknowledgement/rejection codes, retry/idempotency, environments, test credentials) and authorisation to connect; PMS vendor APIs/contracts per provider; an identity/authentication service; a backend with persistent storage, key management and audit store; a hosting/security assessment; legal basis and data-protection approvals for guest data.
**Critical security/operational concerns requiring separate assessment (not assessed here; no certification claimed):** no authentication or server-side authorisation; masking is cosmetic (full document numbers/DOBs are held in browser JavaScript state); audit records are mutable, volatile and unsigned; CSP is a `<meta>` tag only (no server headers); no secrets management, backup, monitoring, incident response or retention model; the demo "Acting as" switch must never ship in a real build.
**Not production-ready.** Passing tests show the prototype behaves as designed and is honest about being simulated; they do not show it meets requirements or is safe to operate.
